Stanford Bunny
Source: Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory, Stanford 3D Scanning Repository
https://graphics.stanford.edu/data/3Dscanrep/

FBX source used by this educational project:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/fbx/stanford-bunny.fbx

Local conversion: scripts/convert-bunny.mjs
The shape was preserved; duplicate vertices were merged and the mesh was saved as binary PLY.
